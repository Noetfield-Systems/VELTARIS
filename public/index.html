Can I try it before requesting a pilot?

Yes. The sample workspace uses illustrative facilities and forecasts. Open the demo.

How is reconciliation handled?

Your team records cleared receipts and matches statement lines manually in CAD. The workspace can export the servicing journal as CSV.

What does the pilot include?

One agreed workflow for one team over four weeks. Scope and fee are confirmed in a written quote.

Who can access an organization?

Organization access is invitation-only. Owner, Editor and Viewer roles determine what each person can do.
