## Founder-led discovery

Before sending, document the recipient's relevance and the basis for contacting them. Include sender identity and unsubscribe instructions where applicable. [ISED consent guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)

### Draft for independent lenders

**Subject** · Time spent preparing your private-credit register

Hi [Name],

I'm building VELTARIS Capital for credit teams that assemble facility registers, maturity schedules and document packets across several tools. I'm interested in how your team produces [specific workflow] and where a correction or missing file holds it up.

Could we spend 20 minutes walking through that workflow with a synthetic example? If there is a fit, we can propose a four-week paid software pilot with one measured outcome.

Sina  
VELTARIS Capital

## Evidence plan

Capture baseline preparation minutes, number of systems and spreadsheet handoffs, correction count, and a principal balance control total agreed with the customer. Repeat the run with the same scope after onboarding. Compare like for like and obtain customer sign-off. For commercial tracking, distinguish discovery, proposal, signed order, issued invoice and reconciled cash. Use the founder's VELTARIS-owned payment arrangement once designated.
