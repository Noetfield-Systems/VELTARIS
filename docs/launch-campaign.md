# VELTARIS Capital launch campaign

## Offer and audience

Promote VELTARIS as private credit operations software for Canadian private credit managers, alternative lenders, and equipment or specialty finance teams. Initial titles include Head of Credit Operations, Portfolio Operations Lead, COO, and Controller. The offer is a four-week pilot scoped to one team and one workflow. The proposed fee is C$2,500, confirmed in writing.

Use the [dedicated campaign page](https://veltaris-capital.vercel.app/private-credit.html). The live funnel is campaign page, interactive sample, pilot request, and private operator follow-up. Source tags are recorded with a successful form submission. The site uses no ad-platform tracking pixel.

## Founder post draft

Private credit teams often need to trace one repayment across the facility file, bank statement, and journal.

VELTARIS brings those steps into one workspace. You can review facilities and documents, record an externally cleared receipt, match a CAD statement line, and export a balanced servicing journal.

We’re opening a four-week software pilot for Canadian private credit and specialty finance teams. It starts with one workflow and an outcome you can measure.

[Try the sample workspace and request a pilot](https://veltaris-capital.vercel.app/private-credit.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=private-credit-launch&utm_content=founder-post)

## Measurement

Review LinkedIn spend, impressions, clicks and click-through rate alongside submitted requests by campaign and creative. In the operator console, review requests for fit and track qualified conversations, proposals, accepted scope and verified receipts separately. Accepted pilot requests are forms successfully stored. Paid conversion requires confirmed payment to a VELTARIS-owned collection arrangement. Use attributed qualified requests and cost per qualified request to compare creative after there is enough signal.

The creatives and founder copy are launch assets. Platform review, campaign activation and delivery are tracked separately.
