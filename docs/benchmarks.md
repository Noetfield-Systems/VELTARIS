# Competitive feature matrix

Baseline reviewed on 2026-11-26. Evidence below describes public capabilities of the named products. VELTARIS status describes the temporary release. Commercial and engineering owners revisit rows after buyer interviews and vendor releases.

| Benchmark | Public capability | VELTARIS release | Next acceptance criterion | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Securitize | Issuance, transfer agency, administration and distribution in its applicable markets | Sample servicing export | Reconcile an issuance and transfer-agent event feed, including correction and duplicate cases | [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2094496/000162828026073937/a424b3-securitizecorpq32026.htm) |
| Tokeny | ERC-3643 permissioned token standard and identity-linked transfers | Off-chain eligibility and transfer simulation | Test sponsor-configured eligibility, blocked transfers and recovery on a non-production chain | [Tokeny docs](https://docs.tokeny.com/docs/overview-1) |
| Centrifuge | Multi-chain fund tokenization and distribution protocol | Sample CAD schedule only | Model subscriptions, share classes and asset-level cutoffs with deterministic reconciliation | [Centrifuge docs](https://docs.centrifuge.io/developer/protocol/overview/) |
| Figure | Loan marketplace and capital market integrations | Sample facility intake and servicing | Validate a loan tape against an agreed buyer schema and record each rejection | [Figure Connect](https://www.figure.com/partner/figure-connect/) |
| Taurus | Tokenization and institutional issuance APIs | Planned | Demonstrate partner adapter reconciliation and documented key ownership in a provider sandbox | [Taurus Developer Hub](https://docs.taurushq.com/protect-capital/reference/tokenization-issuance) |
| Fireblocks | Tokenization engine for deploying and managing tokenized assets | Planned | Round-trip approval, operation ID and webhook replay with a provider sandbox | [Fireblocks Tokenization](https://www.fireblocks.com/products/tokenization) |
| Polymesh | On-chain identity, asset rules and claims | Planned | Compare policy decisions across a permissioned chain and ERC-3643 using the same fixtures | [Polymesh identity](https://developers.polymesh.network/polymesh-docs/primitives/identity/) |
| FrontFundr | Registered dealer and private-market subscription funnel | Private software-pilot request funnel | Map issuer and investor responsibility boundaries with a prospective Canadian partner | [AMF register](https://lautorite.qc.ca/en/general-public/registers/list-of-equity-crowdfunding-portals) |

| Wealthsimple | Client-facing private credit updates and risk communication | Responsive workspace, sample labels, and explicit schedule assumptions | Observe five lender operators completing the same tasks and capture completion time and error rate | [Wealthsimple Advisors](https://insights.wealthsimple.com/private-credit-portfolio-update) |
| Stripe | Hosted payments, invoicing resources and lifecycle events | Pilot scope page | Produce a VELTARIS software invoice and reconcile receipt in the venture's own accounts | [Stripe invoicing API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices) |
| Ondo | On-chain product packaging and distribution | Hypothetical instrument unit calculation | Trace product facts, investor eligibility and distribution scope to versioned disclosures | [Ondo](https://ondo.finance/ousg) |

| Obligate | On-chain debt issuance workflows | Scenario for proposed debt units | Tie terms, settlement, maturity and evidence to the same instrument ID | [Obligate](https://www.obligate.com/services) |
| Maple | On-chain institutional credit underwriting and servicing | Sample interest-only schedule | Validate review steps, collateral provenance and servicing exceptions with a pilot lender | [Maple](https://maple.finance/insights/allocation-strategies) |
| DigiShares | White-label tokenization with configurable tiers | Standard branded site | Prove controlled branding changes and separate tenant configuration | [DigiShares plans](https://digishares.io/pricing) |
| Brickken | Developer integration and issuer tokenization UX | Sample terms and transfer-policy controls | Walk through issuer onboarding and terms review with recorded errors | [Brickken Developer Hub](https://devs.brickken.com/challenges/build-with-brickken) |
| Canton | Institutional network supporting privacy-enabled assets | Off-chain only | Compare privacy boundaries and integration costs against another protocol using one fixture | [Canton](https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/ledger-canton-coin-privacy-enabled-assets) |

| Juniper Square | Private-fund administration and investor reporting | Portfolio sample exports | Verify a controlled investor statement and reporting period with a sample private fund | [Juniper Square](https://pages.junipersquare.com/Fund-Admin.html) |
| Plaid | Holdings and transaction data access | Manual sample facility data | Normalize a financial-data feed with provenance, consent and data-quality errors | [Plaid Investments](https://plaid.com/docs/investments/) |
| Adyen | Enterprise collections, payouts and liquidity reference | Software payment routing undecided | Demonstrate invoice and settlement reconciliation on a VELTARIS-owned PSP | [Adyen](https://www.adyen.com/en_GB/knowledge-hub/best-payments-liquidity-management-platforms-for-enterprises) |
| Ramp | Financial operations and review experience | Facility filtering and export | Validate readability, exception triage, and workflow completion with operators | [Ramp](https://ramp.com/) |

The immediate buyer comparison is Carta's private-credit workflow and Figure's loan-data quality. Partner adapters follow buyer demand and transaction design. Review each Canadian activity against its own roles, facts, and jurisdiction before enabling it. The CSA's tokenization initiative provides primary Canadian context.
