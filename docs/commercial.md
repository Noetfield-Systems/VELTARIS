# Commercial launch and founder-led fundraising

## Paid pilot

Sell a four-week software pilot for one operations team and one workflow. Start with deal intake, servicing visibility, or portfolio exports. Use synthetic data for the initial review. For private customer data, agree access, retention, and tenant boundaries before ingestion.

Proposed validation price in CAD is 2,500 for the implementation and training pilot. Propose 750 monthly for a continuing workspace after acceptance. These are test prices for the founder to validate in live buyer conversations. Compare willingness to pay, procurement time, and support load across buyers. Software fees go to a VELTARIS-designated invoice and accounts.

| Moment | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Buyer role, current workflow, volume, handoff, baseline time, owner and next meeting |
| Qualified | Buyer confirms one recurring workflow, implementation owner, and plausible budget |
| Proposal | Named workflow, deliverables, owner, data scope, price, and measurable acceptance |
| Signed | Executed software pilot order |
| Paid | Invoice and independently reconciled settlement reference |
| Accepted | Verified control total and buyer sign-off on measured improvement |
| Converted | Recurring agreement and first recurring invoice receipt |

Discovery exercise. Have the operator run one synthetic facility through intake, project a maturity payment, and export the register. Agree one measurable improvement, such as producing the weekly register in half the baseline time while reconciling 100% of control totals.

## Founder fundraising

The investment case starts with a Canadian private-credit workflow and recurring software fees. Expansion into issuance, investor operations, and servicing integrations depends on contracts and validated demand. Customer concentration, incumbent integrations, buyer procurement, and regulated-role dependencies are primary diligence questions.

| Research target | Hypothesis and verification step | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Luge Capital | Fintech thesis fits. Confirm current stage range and relevant portfolio conflicts. | [Luge Capital](https://www.luge.vc/?lang=en) |
| Diagram Ventures | Financial technology thesis fits. Determine whether venture investing or venture building is the relevant route. | [Diagram](https://diagram.vc/) |
| Portage | Financial technology investor. Verify partner and stage fit before founder introduction. | [Power Corporation reporting](https://www.powercorporation.com/media/uploads/reports/quarter/bpcc-2025-q2-eng-web-final_EH1jDrp.pdf) |

Private data room index for founder completion. Incorporation and ownership records, IP assignments and dependency inventory, financial statements and ledger, pilot orders and receipts, buyer interview evidence, product and test evidence, security and data flows, integration and legal memos, financial model, financing terms, and material agreements. Record owner, primary evidence, visibility and date on each item. Keep customer records and diligence documents outside this public repository.

### Planning scenarios, 12 months, CAD

| Assumption or output | Downside | Base | Upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paid pilots | 3 | 12 | 24 |
| Continuing customers | 1 | 6 | 15 |
| Average paid subscription months per conversion | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| Pilot fees at 2,500 each | 7,500 | 30,000 | 60,000 |
| Subscription fees at 750/month | 3,000 | 22,500 | 67,500 |
| Gross contracted fee scenario, before collection timing | 10,500 | 52,500 | 127,500 |
| Ending monthly recurring revenue run-rate | 750 | 4,500 | 11,250 |

Each scenario assumes fully paid pilot and subscription amounts and zero churn among listed continuing customers. Actual cash forecasting requires invoice terms, payment timing, refunds, costs and taxes. The VELTARIS software forecast excludes customer lending volume, securities proceeds, custody, distribution fees, and token economics.
